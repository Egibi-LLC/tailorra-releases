tailorra

A Windows desktop app that tailors your resume to each job listing.

You keep one master copy of your work history. For each listing, tailorra drafts a targeted resume and cover letter with AI suggestions you review before anything changes, then exports to PDF, DOCX, HTML, Markdown, or plain text.

Download for Windows

Latest version, 64-bit installer for Windows 10 and 11. Or browse all releases.

Linux (beta): AppImage or deb, x86-64. The AppImage auto-updates; the deb does not.

SmartScreen warning: the installer is not yet signed with an Authenticode certificate, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" when you run it. Click More info, then Run anyway. Releases are minisign-signed and the app verifies the signature on every update it installs. Every release also ships a PGP-signed checksum manifest; see Verify your download.
